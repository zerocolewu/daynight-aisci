5. NON-MONOTONIC RELATIONSHIP
================================================================================

Corruption Rate -> Test RMSE shows a non-monotonic pattern:
  25% -> 51.53
  40% -> 54.06  (WORSE)
  50% -> 49.83  (BEST)
  60% -> 51.71  (improves again but not as good as 50%)

This non-monotonicity is unusual and suggests:
1. At 40%, the corruption is "the worst of both worlds" - enough to
   hurt performance but not enough to force robust feature learning
2. At 50%, there's a phase transition where the model develops
   genuinely useful invariances
3. At 60%, the model finds shortcuts in heavily corrupted views

The 50% rate may correspond to a critical threshold where ~half the
graph is corrupted, forcing the model to learn from the remaining
half - which aligns with learning from partial polymer structure.
