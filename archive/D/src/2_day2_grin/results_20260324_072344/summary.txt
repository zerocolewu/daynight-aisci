5. MEAN_AUG: Mean aggregation + {1,3}-RU augmentation
   - Status: NOT RUN (previous experiment failed)

Key Findings:
-------------
- The baseline model (mean aggregation, no augmentation) performed BEST with Test RMSE of 76.08
- Max aggregation alone performed slightly worse (RMSE: 78.75)
- Adding L1 sparsity to max aggregation showed minor improvement (RMSE: 77.94 vs 78.75)
- The augmentation feature has a critical bug that prevents training completion

Recommendations:
----------------
1. Fix the augmentation pipeline to handle graph conversion failures gracefully
2. Ensure batch sizes remain consistent even when some graphs fail to convert
3. Consider using drop_last=True in DataLoader to avoid partial batches
4. Add better error handling for SMILES-to-graph conversion
5. Investigate why 3-RU augmentation is failing (possibly invalid SMILES generation)
