   Test RMSE: 45.56  ← BEST ablation result!

3. No non-covalent edges (covalent only):
   Test RMSE: 48.34  ← Better than full model
   Train-Val Gap: -1.8 (slight underfitting)
   Conclusion: Non-covalent edges may be adding noise

❌ CHALLENGES:

- Less is more in this case

================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

PolyKAN-SSM v4 successfully achieved its primary goal: eliminating overfitting.
The train-val gap of 0.55 is excellent compared to v3's 11.2. However, the
absolute performance (RMSE 54.54) is worse than v3 (~40) and far from SOTA (30.8).

The ablation studies provide clear direction: simpler is better. The best
performing variant used standard GINConv without Fourier-KAN, suggesting that
the complex SSM and Fourier formulations are over-engineering for this task.

Next step: v5 should combine the best elements - GINConv backbone, strong
regularization, moderate capacity, and simplified architecture.
